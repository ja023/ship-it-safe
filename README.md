# Ship It Safe

**A production-readiness checklist for vibe-coded web apps.**

AI coding tools will happily build you a working app in a weekend. What they won't do unless you ask is the unglamorous stuff that keeps it alive in production: auth that holds up, secrets that stay secret, payments that can't be faked, backups that actually restore, and the legal basics.

This repo is a single checklist covering all of it, in plain language, organized by area.

👉 **[Open the checklist](CHECKLIST.md)**

  - Career

## How to use it

- **Manually:** go section by section and tick off what's done. Skip what doesn't apply to your stack.
- **With an AI assistant:** give it `CHECKLIST.md` and your codebase and ask it to audit the app against the checklist, reporting pass / fail / not applicable for each item with the fixes ranked by risk.

## Disclaimer

This is general guidance, not legal or security advice. Prices, platform limits and laws change, so verify anything involving a number, a deadline or a legal requirement before relying on it.
